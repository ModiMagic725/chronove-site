How to read the engine

The case is the outermost layer: bevelled, brushed and shaped to hold everything below it. Open the view and you will see its bezel catch the light separately from the flanks.

Below it sits the movement, the geartrain and the balance that count time. The gears turn at their true relationships, and the balance swings back and forth, slowed here so you can follow it.

The bridge holds the wheels in line. It is the part a watchmaker spends longest finishing, because it is the part you see when the caseback is open.

Finally the dial and the hands, driven by your own clock. Nothing here is pre-recorded: the seconds hand is the time where you are.
