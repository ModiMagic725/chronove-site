What we kept

Through every collection the same three habits survived: draw the dial before the case, finish the parts nobody sees, and never add a complication that cannot be explained in a sentence.

Early prototypes still sit in a drawer at the workshop, wrapped in cloth. They are not sold and not displayed. They are reminders that every good watch began as a less good one.
