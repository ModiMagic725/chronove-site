Notes from the bench

No metal is best at everything. Titanium is light and warm to the touch but marks more easily; steel is heavier and takes the sharpest polish; ceramic resists scratches almost completely but can chip on a hard blow.

The ratings shown here are comparative, not laboratory measurements. They describe how the materials behave on the wrist, in the order a watchmaker would rank them.

Sapphire is used for every crystal in the collection, because a dial that stays clear for decades matters more than almost any other detail.
