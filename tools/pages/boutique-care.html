Looking after a watch

A mechanical watch is a long relationship. These are the questions we hear most.

How often should it be serviced?

Every five to seven years for most calibres. Heavy daily use or diving may call for a check every three. Each service includes a full strip-down, cleaning, oiling and regulation.

What does the warranty cover?

Five years on the movement and case against defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover damage from impact, water ingress through an open crown, or modification by a third party.

Can I change the strap myself?

Yes. Every strap has quick-release spring bars. A small pointed tool is included. Bracelets are sized in the boutique so that the clasp sits correctly.

How should I store it?

Away from strong magnets and from heat, ideally in a dry drawer. A self-winding watch that is not worn for a week should be wound a few turns and reset before use.

Is it water resistant?

Ratings are given on each watch page. Water resistance depends on seals that age, so we recommend a pressure test with every service. Never operate the crown or pushers underwater.
