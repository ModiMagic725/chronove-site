Why add a complication

Every complication adds parts, and every part is a place for something to go wrong. The maison adds one only when it earns its place on the wrist.

A calendar spares you from checking a phone. A moon phase connects you to the night sky. A countdown timer is useful if you race boats, and beautiful if you do not.

The ones in this collection were chosen for their honesty: what the dial shows is what the mechanism does, and nothing is faked.
