Choosing between them

Start with the wrist, not the movement. A 38 mm Meridian wears close and quiet. A 42 mm Tidal 300 has presence and weighs more on the wrist even in titanium.

Then decide how much of the mechanism you want to see. Skeleton watches show everything; classic dials show the hour and trust the rest to the caseback.

Complications are about habit. If you like to set a date once a year, Equinox is for you. If you would rather never touch it, choose Perpetua.
