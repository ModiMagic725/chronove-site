Equinox, one turn at a time

40 mm

The watch

Champagne gold, polished on the bezel and satin-brushed everywhere else, so the metal glows instead of glaring.

11.6 mm

The profile

A domed sapphire crystal sits above a case cut slim enough for a cuff, with enough height to carry an annual calendar.

122 yrs

The dial

A gold moon turns on a midnight enamel disc, and slips one day behind in about a hundred and twenty-two years.

60 h

The movement

Calibre CV-C4 beats four times a second, with 33 jewels and sixty hours in reserve. You can see it through the caseback.
